What ClearCrest does

ClearCrest Water & Air installs and maintains whole-home water softening, reverse osmosis and certified PFAS drinking water filtration, UV disinfection, and whole-house air purification (MERV 13 to 16 media air cleaners and in-duct UV) for Utah homeowners. Utah water commonly tests 15 to 30+ grains per gallon, which is far into the very hard range.

Service area

Park City and the Heber Valley, Eagle Mountain and Saratoga Springs, South Jordan and Daybreak, Lehi and north Utah County, the greater Salt Lake Valley, Davis and Weber counties, and St. George.

Pricing

The in-home water and air test is free, takes about 20 minutes, and produces a written ClearCrest Home Report for the exact address. Whole-home water systems start from $5,000 installed with consumer financing available. CrestCare maintenance memberships start from $29 per month (Essential), $59 per month (Complete, water and air), and $99 per month (Concierge, including same-day emergency service and transferable coverage).
