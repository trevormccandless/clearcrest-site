Information we collect

When you book a free in-home test or contact us, we collect the information you provide: name, email, phone, address or community, and any notes about your water and air goals. Our website uses standard analytics to understand aggregate traffic.

How we use it

We use your information to schedule and perform your in-home test, prepare your ClearCrest Home Report, provide quotes and service, manage CrestCare memberships, and communicate with you about appointments. Booking submissions are processed through a form delivery service that routes your message to our team.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties except service providers who help us operate (such as our form delivery and scheduling providers) and as required by law.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by emailing hello@clearcresthome.com. You can opt out of marketing communications using the unsubscribe link in any email.
